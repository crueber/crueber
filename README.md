### 👨‍💻 Open Source:

#### Self-hosted and home-lab oriented

- [passage](https://github.com/crueber/passage) (web, go, oauth, htmx, docker) - OIDC for your home lab. Password, Passkey, Magic Link. MFA.
- [loom](https://github.com/crueber/loom) (web, go, solidjs, docker) - Your new self-hosted "home" page. Links, notes, and more. Take back your new tab.
- [weave](https://github.com/crueber/weave) (web, go, htmx, docker) - Kanban project management for humans and agents. 
- [walhub](https://github.com/crueber/walhub) (web, go, solidjs, docker) - alpha, do not use - Object storage based git store; Inspired by [walgit](https://github.com/tobi/walgit).

#### Stand alone apps

- [coldstorage](https://github.com/crueber/coldstorage) (tui, go, git) - stable - terminal dashboard for all your local git repositories; Inspired by [drydock](https://github.com/yetidevworks/drydock).
- [android-brightness](https://github.com/crueber/android-brightness) (android, kotlin, widget) - stable - Just a minimal brightness widget for Android's home screen.

#### Statically Hosted Apps

- [starship](https://github.com/crueber/starship) 💫 - Fly a little starship from Earth to the nearest stars, [right in your browser](https://crueber.github.io/starship/).
- [hamplitude](https://github.com/crueber/hamplitude) 📻 - Compendium and concept-first study site for US amateur radio exams. [hamplitude.net](https://hamplitude.net)
